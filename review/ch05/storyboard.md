## b712 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Now let me tell you what this means.

## b713 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Some individuals can attain fullness of understanding spontaneously.

## b714 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Without needing another method.

## b715 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Their conceptual understanding becomes complete through its own refinement.

## b716 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Lovely.

## b717 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

For others, understanding needs assistance.

## b718 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

And that assistance takes many forms.

## b719 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Through the intellect.

## b720 · verse · verse · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Dhī.

## b721 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Through vital energy.

## b722 · verse · verse · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Prāṇa.

## b723 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Through the body.

## b724 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Deha.

## b725 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

And through external objects.

## b726 · verse · verse · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Bahiḥ.

## b727 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

These are classified under āṇavopāya because they rely upon limited supports.

## b728 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

But here’s the final point.

## b729 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

In relation to the supreme result, there is no difference between these methods.

## b730 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Now that’s remarkable.

## b731 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Because you might have spent the entire book thinking:

“Well, anupāya is obviously the best.”

## b733 · dialogue · man · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

“Śāmbhavopāya must be better than Śāktopāya.”

“And āṇavopāya sounds a bit rubbish, doesn’t it?”

After all, it’s the method for the limited individual.

## b736 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Surely that’s the bottom of the pile?

## b737 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

And Abhinavagupta says:

## b738 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

You’re missing the point.

## b739 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

The methods differ according to the capacities and conditions of their practitioners.

## b740 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

But the ultimate realisation?

## b741 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

That’s not divided.

## b742 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

You don’t get a slightly worse Śiva because you needed breathwork.

## b743 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

You don’t get a budget version of liberation because you started with meditation.

## b744 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

And the person who recognises through a limited support is not thereby recognising a lesser supreme consciousness.

## b745 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

The fruit is one.

## b746 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Para-phala.

## b747 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

The supreme result.

## b748 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

And what is that?

## b749 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

The recognition of the unrestricted consciousness that is your own essential nature.

## b750 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Now consider the whole journey.

## b751 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

You began with a limited understanding.

## b752 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

You thought:

“I am this body.”

## b754 · dialogue · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

“I am this mind.”

## b755 · dialogue · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

“I am this individual.”

So you used the body.

## b757 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

You used the mind.

## b758 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

You used the breath.

## b759 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

You used sound.

## b760 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

You used visualisation.

## b761 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

You examined objects appearing in awareness.

## b762 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

You watched the movements through which they arose and disappeared.

## b763 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

## b764 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

The body wasn’t the enemy.

## b765 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Breathing wasn’t the enemy.

## b766 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Thinking wasn’t the enemy.

## b769 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

## b770 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Śiva was never absent from the limited individual.

## b771 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

## b772 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

## b773 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

## b776 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Looking at a flower.

## b777 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Hearing a sound.

## b778 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

## b779 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

You thought you needed to escape your embodied condition to find Śiva.

## b780 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

## b781 · narration · granny · [Tantrasāra — Chapter 5]

Absolutely magnificent.
