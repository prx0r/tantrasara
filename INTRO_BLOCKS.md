# INTRO BLOCKS — how long, what you see, how to amend

All 22 chapters now share one intro shape. Intro is blocks b1 through the
handoff, opener excluded. It is conventional, measured, never composed.

## Shape

- ch01: b1 title granny, b2-b7 overview granny (role, Essence tagline,
  Tantraloka setup, same-teaching handoff, homage), b7 handoff Right.
  Lets get into it. 110 words to handoff, 146 with the mangala verses.
  Visual thumbnail throughout, verse cards for b8-b10 only.
- ch02-22: b1 spoken number granny, b2 Sanskrit name verse, b3 English
  tagline granny, b4 beat Right then, b5 onward recap Last chapter plus
  2-4 beats. Twelve-block sample runs 70-101 words. Visual thumbnail from
  frame 0 through the intro (first tone break — GATES H1, owner call
  2026-11-04). b2 chants as standard since 2026-11-04 (overrides
  cleared all 21; ch01 b2 stays granny English). Forcing b2 to granny
  remains available per chapter by override. Machine never decides.

## Budget

Title plus role plus overview plus handoff, verse excluded, at or under
150 words. Recap at or under 5 beats. No repeated greeting formulae, each
handoff in its own words. Current flags: ch06 101 in twelve blocks but
217 by the chapter-format count, ch14 98 in twelve blocks but 179 flagged —
  owner trim-or-exception, machine never cuts prose.

## Visuals

Thumbnail fits above the subtitle zone, never fullscreen bleed, holds
until the first body chant starts. Verse cards Devanagari only, max three
pairs per hold, wrapped 64 when needed, assert no overflow. Term cards
Devanagari headword plus confirmed grey gloss, popping the instant she
says the word, newer cue supersedes. Proposed glosses play over white.
White returns at every tone break, section break, long-pause beat and
dialogue entry. No title cards in video. Subtitles Cormorant 46 to 40 at
y 860 max two lines, ink lit grey unlit, IAST for verse blocks.

## Amending

To amend an intro visual, add the bid to that chapter overrides with the
wanted voice and a one-line reason, rebuild green, republish the review
pack. b2 to granny keeps thumbnail through the name. Shortening a recap
is a source edit plus commit, then board plus cast-check plus publish
again. Approval pins storyboard and glossary SHAs — any change needs a
re-bless before render refuses lift.
