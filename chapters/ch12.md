The Sun.

You can perform mantra-nyāsa.

You can engage in internal contemplation.

And all these may serve as supports.

But the highest bath?

Immersion in Parabhairava.

The supreme consciousness that is never separate from your own essential being.

And that’s the punchline.

You thought you needed to wash something off yourself to reach Śiva.

Abhinavagupta says the very conviction that you’re something other than Śiva is the impurity.

You don’t wash yourself until you become Śiva. You recognise that even the apparent impurity never possessed an independent reality outside Śiva.

And that recognition is the real purification.

Absolutely bloody magnificent.

End of Chapter Twelve — Snānaprakāśanam.
