# Śiva Sūtras 1.10

अविवेको मायासौषुप्तम्

Aviveko māyāsauṣuptam.

Non-discernment is the deep sleep associated with Māyā.

Deep Sleep: No Thoughts, Still Not Enlightened.

Now we’ve reached the third condition.

No ordinary waking perception.

No elaborate dream imagery.

No conceptual soap opera.

Just profound non-discernment.

You might be tempted to conclude:

“Excellent. No thoughts, no suffering, no separation. Therefore deep sleep is enlightenment.”

Unfortunately, Vasugupta is not having it.

Because the absence of differentiated experience is not automatically the recognition of your true nature.

A person emerging from dreamless sleep ordinarily hasn’t acquired the knowledge:

“I am universal consciousness, intrinsically free, the source of manifestation.”

They’ve acquired the knowledge:

“Where’s my phone?”

The subtle distinction is between the absence of manifest differentiation and the positive recognition of nondual awareness.

One is a condition in which distinctions are unmanifest.

The other is recognition of the conscious reality underlying all distinctions, whether they are manifest or not.

And Māyā remains relevant because non-discernment does not itself undo the structures of limitation.

This is why Trika refuses to equate enlightenment with unconsciousness, blankness, or simply suppressing thought.

You cannot become Śiva by knocking yourself unconscious.

Terrible news for anyone hoping to attain liberation through an exceptionally long afternoon nap.
