# Śiva Sūtras 1.20

भूतसन्धानभूतपृथक्त्वविश्वसङ्घट्टाः

Bhūtasandhāna-bhūtapṛthaktva-viśvasaṅghaṭṭāḥ.

The joining of existents, their separation, and the bringing together of the universe.

Congratulations, You’re Now Playing Minecraft With Reality.

Here come more siddhis.

Three broad forms of extraordinary accomplishment are presented.

First, bhūtasandhāna.

The bringing together or integration of existent things.

Second, bhūtapṛthaktva.

Their separation or differentiation.

Third, viśvasaṅghaṭṭa.

The bringing together of things throughout the universe.

Kṣemarāja associates these with powers affecting bodies and other entities, including their integration, separation, and the apprehension of otherwise distant things.

Yes, the actual traditional interpretation includes extraordinary abilities. Sanskrit & Trika Shaivism.

But what is their philosophical significance?

Return to the beginning.

The universe manifests through Śakti.

Differentiation arises through the powers of consciousness.

And limitations associated with space, time, agency and knowledge constitute the contracted experience of the individual.

From the standpoint of the tradition, a yogi established in the supreme source of those powers is no longer understood as subject to ordinary limitation in the same way.

Hence the claims about siddhis.

But there is an important hierarchy here.

The extraordinary abilities are not the fundamental definition of liberation.

Recognition of one’s true nature is more fundamental than the acquisition of unusual capabilities.

Otherwise, the goal of spiritual life would simply become accumulating supernatural party tricks.

You could assemble worlds, separate elements, perceive distant things—

And still spend your afternoon being annoyed because somebody didn’t reply to your message.

Quite an achievement.
