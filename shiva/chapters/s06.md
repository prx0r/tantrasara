# Śiva Sūtras 1.6

शक्तिचक्रसन्धाने विश्वसंहारः

Śakticakrasandhāne viśvasaṃhāraḥ.

Through union with the circle of powers, the universe is withdrawn.

The Universe Gets Swallowed, but Don’t Worry About the Furniture.

Right.

Now we’re dissolving universes.

Six statements in.

Excellent pace.

Śakti is power.

Cakra is a wheel, circle, or collective.

Sandhāna means joining, connecting, or bringing into union.

Viśvasaṃhāra is the withdrawal or dissolution of the universe.

But we’re not saying the advanced yogi sits in his bedroom and Jupiter physically disappears.

Rather, Kṣemarāja is explaining the withdrawal of differentiated manifestation into its conscious source.

Consider all the powers constituting your experience.

Seeing.

Hearing.

Thinking.

Remembering.

Imagining.

Desiring.

Each appears as a distinct activity.

Ordinarily, attention follows these activities outward, towards their objects.

But what happens when consciousness recognises the shared reality of these powers?

The apparent separateness of their manifestations is reabsorbed into the unity from which they arise.

The universe is no longer experienced as something independent of the conscious reality manifesting it.

This is saṃhāra — withdrawal.

And it’s inseparable from manifestation.

Śiva expresses the universe through Śakti.

Śiva withdraws the universe through Śakti.

It’s not a one-way escape from a terrible material world.

It’s a dynamic movement of manifestation and reabsorption within consciousness itself.

Like a wave discovering that it was never separate from the ocean.

Except the ocean is conscious, infinitely free, and has been pretending to be every single wave.

Which is quite the hobby.
