# Śiva Sūtras 1.21

शुद्धविद्योदयाच्चक्रेशत्वसिद्धिः

Śuddhavidyodayāc cakreśatvasiddhiḥ.

Through the arising of pure knowledge, mastery over the circle of powers is attained.

You’ve Been Promoted to Manager of the Entire Śakti Department.

Now Vasugupta connects spiritual sovereignty to śuddhavidyā.

Pure knowledge.

Importantly, this is not simply a person becoming exceptionally well educated.

Nor is it a collection of correct philosophical opinions.

Śuddhavidyā is also a technical category within the Trika system of the thirty-six tattvas.

The five pure tattvas are:

Śiva
Śakti
Sadāśiva
Īśvara
Śuddhavidyā
They describe pure modalities of consciousness and its manifestation before the limitations associated with Māyā and the contracted individual.

Within this pure domain, the relation between aham — I — and idam — this — is not experienced through the same fundamental alienation that characterises ordinary differentiated existence.

And that’s the essential point.

At the beginning, the individual was bound by limited knowing.

Now pure knowledge has arisen.

The yogi recognises the fundamental relation between the conscious subject and its powers of manifestation.

And this leads to cakreśatva.

Lordship over the circle of powers.

Remember our previous encounter with śakticakra?

The wheel or collective of powers.

At 1.6, their union was associated with the withdrawal of the universe.

Here, the rise of pure knowledge is associated with mastery over them.

The progression is deliberate.

The powers that once seemed to scatter your consciousness into countless apparently separate objects are no longer experienced as fundamentally alien to their source.

The master of the powers is not an independent ego controlling an external collection of cosmic instruments.

The sovereign reality is Śiva himself.

And the realised yogi recognises their identity with that reality.
