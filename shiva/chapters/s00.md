# The Śiva Sūtras

Vasugupta’s Guide to Realising You’re Literally Śiva, You Absolute Donut.

The Way of Śiva: Your Consciousness Has Been Running the Entire Universe and Somehow You Didn’t Notice.

Right. We’re back in Kashmir.

Ninth century, give or take. The Himalayas. Snow-covered mountains. Ancient temples. A man named Vasugupta is about to receive what tradition describes as a revelation from Śiva himself.

According to one famous account, Śiva appears to Vasugupta in a dream and directs him towards a rock bearing a secret teaching.

And what’s this incredible revelation?

Seventy-seven tiny statements.

That’s it.

Twenty-two in the first section. Ten in the second. Forty-five in the third.

No elaborate mythology. No 600-page introduction. Just the metaphysical equivalent of a bloke walking into your house, informing you that you’ve misunderstood the entire nature of reality, and disappearing.

These are the Śiva Sūtras.

And unlike the Tantrasāra, which was written by Abhinavagupta as an extensive systematic exposition, this thing is absolutely compressed.

A single word can contain an entire metaphysics.

Fortunately, Abhinavagupta’s disciple Kṣemarāja wrote the Śivasūtravimarśinī, which helps unpack what on earth Vasugupta is on about.

We’re going through all 77 in their traditional order, with the authentic Sanskrit, accessible glosses, and an original, mildly unhinged narration grounded primarily in Kṣemarāja’s interpretation. The jokes are ours; the sūtras are real.

Lets get into it.
