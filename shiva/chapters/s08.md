# Śiva Sūtras 1.8

ज्ञानं जाग्रत्

Jñānaṃ jāgrat.

Knowledge is waking.

The Waking World Is Just One Department.

Specifically, the relevant form of jñāna is cognition of the externally presented world.

You open your eyes.

There’s a cup.

There’s a table.

There’s a gecko on the wall looking suspiciously like he owns the property.

Everything appears organised into objects apprehended through the senses.

This is waking consciousness.

But do not confuse the waking state’s relative clarity with ultimate awakening.

You can be wide awake physically and completely unaware of your own fundamental nature.

You’ve got your eyes open.

You’ve got eighteen tabs open.

You’re analysing interest rates and arguing about whether somebody has used a Sanskrit compound correctly.

And the whole time, the consciousness in which all these things are appearing remains unrecognised.

You’re very busy knowing things.

But you’ve forgotten the knower.
